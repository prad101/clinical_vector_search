Example Queries & Responses

What are the common symptoms of Type 2 diabetes?
Type 2 diabetes commonly presents with increased thirst, frequent urination, increased hunger, fatigue, blurred vision, slow-healing sores, and frequent infections. Some patients may also experience areas of darkened skin, particularly in the armpits and neck.
Recommended treatment protocols for hypertension in elderly patients
For elderly patients with hypertension, treatment typically begins with lifestyle modifications including sodium restriction and regular exercise. First-line pharmacotherapy often includes thiazide diuretics or calcium channel blockers, with target blood pressure of <140/90 mmHg, individualized based on comorbidities and frailty status.
What are the contraindications for MRI scanning?
Absolute contraindications include cardiac pacemakers, implantable cardioverter-defibrillators, cochlear implants, and metallic foreign bodies in the eyes. Relative contraindications include claustrophobia, first trimester pregnancy, and certain prosthetic heart valves. Patient screening is essential before any MRI procedure.
